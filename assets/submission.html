📋 Project Summary * 184 Words (50-200 Words Rule)
Project tellai is an AI-augmented decision-support platform developed to help smallholder farmers and extensionists transition from vulnerable monocultures to resilient, regenerative crop rotations and polycultures. Addressing the "Field Shift" challenge, tellai integrates 24 years of NASA Earth Observations—including NASA POWER agroclimatology (precipitation, temperature, and clear-sky solar radiation), SMAP root-zone soil moisture, MODIS vegetation persistence, and SRTM topography—with local soil data, ZARC agroclimatic risk zoning, and municipal agricultural statistics from IBGE. By calculating the Land Equivalent Ratio (LER > 1.35) and modeling multi-season rotations through mixed-integer optimization, tellai evaluates soil cover during the dry season, mitigates thermal stress, and recommends companion planting strategies that biologically fix nitrogen. tellai is vital because smallholder farmers produce over 70% of Brazil’s staple food, yet only 8.2% in the semi-arid region receive regular technical assistance. Under erratic rainfall and extreme heat, monoculture failure threatens community food security. tellai bridges cutting-edge space science with traditional agroecological wisdom, empowering farmers to conserve up to 45% of soil moisture, restore soil organic carbon, and safeguard livelihoods against climate change (directly advancing UN SDGs 2, 6, 12, 13, and 15).
🔬 Project Details *

🛰️ What does tellai do and how does it work?

tellai is an open-source, intelligent decision-support platform designed to help smallholder farmers and agricultural extensionists in semi-arid and drought-prone regions transition from vulnerable monocultures to climate-resilient, regenerative crop rotations and polycultures.

Operating under the guiding philosophy "supports, does not decide", tellai works by harmonizing 24 years of NASA Earth Observations across 195 semi-arid locations with local soil parameters, official Brazilian agricultural statistics (IBGE), and agroclimatic risk zoning (ZARC):

  1. Satellite Diagnostic Pipeline: Ingests daily NASA POWER agroclimatology (rainfall, extreme temperatures, and all-sky vs. clear-sky solar irradiance), NASA SMAP root-zone soil moisture, NASA SRTM topographical slope, and MODIS vegetation indices (NDVI) to identify historical dry spells (veranicos) and critical bare-soil exposure windows (Campo Nu).
  2. Machine Learning & Uncertainty Modeling: Deploys an XGBoost predictive model calibrated with Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI 80% and 90% coverage), achieving a 28% error reduction over historical climatology in forecasting vegetation anomalies.
  3. Agroecological Optimization: Uses multi-criteria stochastic sorting (SMAA-O) and Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) to generate multi-season crop rotation plans and intercropping strategies based on peer-reviewed Land Equivalent Ratio (LER > 1.35) studies.
  4. Accessible Interfaces: Delivers insights through two tailored interfaces: an interactive diagnostic dashboard (Streamlit) for detailed technical simulation, and Maíra, an accessible, voice-enabled conversational AI (React 19 + FastAPI + DeepSeek) grounded in a strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system with automated fact-checking guardrails.

🌱 What benefits does it have?

  • Food & Income Security: Replaces single-crop failure risks with diversified year-round harvests (proteins from cowpea/pigeon pea, energy from corn/cassava, and forage from spineless cactus).
  • Soil Water Conservation: Living mulches (such as squash and spreading legumes) cut direct soil evaporation by 40% to 60% under intense solar radiation (>20 MJ/m²/day).
  • Zero Synthetic Nitrogen Dependency: Integrates biological nitrogen fixation (BNF) through symbiotic Rhizobium legumes, reducing costly and polluting fossil-fuel fertilizers.
  • Extensionist Empowerment: Empowers local extension agents with automated, longitudinal environmental profiles for farms, preparing high-quality on-field technical recommendations.

🎯 What is the intended impact of the project?

In the Brazilian Semi-Arid region, smallholder farming accounts for more than 70% of staple food production, yet only 8.2% of rural establishments receive regular technical assistance (IBGE Agricultural Census). Climate change has intensified rainfall irregularities and topsoil degradation, pushing vulnerable communities toward food insecurity.

tellai directly addresses this crisis by advancing five UN Sustainable Development Goals:

  • SDG 2 (Zero Hunger - Target 2.4): Increases harvest yield per hectare with Land Equivalent Ratio (LER > 1.35) without requiring deforestation.
  • SDG 6 (Clean Water - Target 6.4): Preserves precious root-zone moisture measured by NASA SMAP and relieves pressure on regional aquifers.
  • SDG 12 (Responsible Production - Target 12.4): Eliminates chemical fertilizer runoff and promotes closed-loop organic cycling.
  • SDG 13 (Climate Action - Target 13.1): Sequesters Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) and buffers canopy temperatures by up to 3.8°C (monitored by ECOSTRESS).
  • SDG 15 (Life on Land - Target 15.3): Halts Caatinga desertification and topsoil runoff on fragile slopes.

💻 What tools, coding languages, hardware, or software did you use?

  • Backend & Scientific Computing: Python 3.14, FastAPI, Uvicorn, XGBoost, Scikit-learn, Scipy, Pandas, NumPy, PyArrow, PyTorch (CPU).
  • Machine Learning & Time Series: Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI), GroupKFold cross-validation by geographic cell, and walk-forward backtesting.
  • Natural Language Processing & RAG: DeepSeek LLM, Hugging Face transformers (multilingual-e5-small), custom hybrid semantic-lexical search (BM25) over 50 regional agronomic manuals, and strict anti-hallucination verification filters.
  • Frontend Web & UI: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Router & Query, Tailwind CSS v4, Three.js / React Three Fiber (3D interactive field and topographical scenes), and Streamlit.
  • Platform Engineering, SRE & GitOps: Multi-stage Docker builds, Nginx Alpine, Docker Compose, automated pre-commit security pipelines, and non-root read-only container hardening.
  • Data Sources & APIs: NASA POWER API, NASA SMAP, NASA/USGS MODIS (ORNL DAAC), NASA SRTM, ISRIC SoilGrids, Brazilian IBGE SIDRA (PAM API), and MAPA ZARC.

💡 How is tellai creative?

  • Prescriptive, Not Just Descriptive: Most environmental tools are passive data viewers. tellai acts as a prescriptive recommendation engine that translates orbital physics into actionable farming practices.
  • Fusing Orbital Tech with Ancestral Knowledge: It unites modern satellite radiometry with proven indigenous polyculture systems (such as the 4,000-year-old Milpa and Cactus CAM metabolism).
  • Radical Scientific Honesty: In an era of black-box AI, tellai refuses to hallucinate precision. It transparently communicates data resolution limits, highlights empirical ties, and explicitly lists data gaps (e.g., market price volatility or seed availability).
  • Voice-First Rural Accessibility: By integrating natural voice interaction in local Brazilian Portuguese, tellai removes technological barriers for farmers with limited literacy.

🔍 What factors did your team consider?

  1. Semi-Arid Biophysical Constraints: The strict 4-month wet season (Quadra Chuvosa, Feb–May), unpredictable multi-week dry spells (veranicos), and the severe Campo Nu dry period.
  2. Edaphic Heterogeneity: Marked soil contrasts across the Cariri region—from shallow, rocky Neossolos susceptible to rapid erosion to deeper Latossolos on the Araripe Plateau.
  3. Socioeconomic Reality: Low capital reserves of smallholders, making expensive synthetic inputs unviable and multi-functional crops (food + animal forage) essential.
  4. Data Sovereignty & Offline Resilience: Ensuring all algorithms can run locally inside containerized environments without perpetual internet dependencies.
